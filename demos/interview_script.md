# 90-second script

"This is a workforce planning agent. Same tools I would use in FP&A: roster, open reqs, burden, cap."

Run **Can we fund the reqs?**

"Base plan fills all six reqs and misses the $1.07M cap by about $31k.
Drop the two nice-to-haves — Austin data analyst in January and recruiter in February — and we are back inside the cap.
November must-hires stay: Senior FP&A and Analytics Engineer."

If they want more, run **Freeze vs grow**.

"A full freeze saves the most money and leaves us under 10 FTE after attrition.
That is a cost answer, not an operating answer. Must-only is the adult version."

Do not say the search is live web. The numbers come from `workspace/` CSVs.
